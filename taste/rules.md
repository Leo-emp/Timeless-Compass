# Timeless Compass owner rules

Read before every stage (`python -m tc rules --stage <stage>`).

## r1 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Narrator leads; characters may speak (Fish voices, library/voices.json) wherever a line fits the moment and boosts the video's vibe (drama, menace, emotion); the narrator still carries the facts; see r12 for dramatised vs sourced lines
- **Why:** owner 2026-11-19 (replaces 'narrator only'): Fish character voices approved; 'we allow character lines where they fit and boost the video vibe'
- **Applies to:** script, voice, shots

## r2 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Serious, dramatic, mysterious tone; never funny or cheesy
- **Why:** AI voices can't do comedy; serious fits history + secret projects
- **Applies to:** script

## r3 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** No on-screen captions or speech bubbles; only place/date cards and map labels
- **Why:** the voice tells the story, like the reference films
- **Applies to:** shots, edit

## r4 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Era accuracy: costumes, armour, gods, soldiers, buildings and landscapes must match the exact time and place
- **Why:** owner requirement; wrong-era details break trust
- **Applies to:** all

## r5 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Growth first: every choice (topic, title, thumbnail, hook, pacing) is ranked by views, CTR and retention
- **Why:** owner priority from day one
- **Applies to:** all

## r6 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Real people are shown through costume, setting and a name card, never as exact face copies
- **Why:** avoid deepfake-style lookalikes
- **Applies to:** shots, build

## r7 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Zero spend: free tiers and free tools only; check pricing before using any paid feature
- **Why:** owner does not want to spend money
- **Applies to:** all

## r8 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Laptop stays light: big files go to R2/cloud; delete local job files after upload
- **Why:** laptop has little disk space
- **Applies to:** render, archive

## r9 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Never copy other productions (e.g. Heroes of Bronze): their frames are only a quality ruler. Character faces, costumes, shield art, shot compositions and colour style are designed from historical sources (busts, vase paintings, museum pieces) and our own settings
- **Why:** owner: we must not copy them; our look must be our own
- **Applies to:** build, shots, library

## r10 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Every shot passes 'tc look' (score >= 80 vs the closest-mood reference frames): keep real deep shadows (3-17% of the frame near black), do not over-brighten faces, keep dusk warmth/saturation inside the reference range
- **Why:** measured 2026-11-19: our renders had 0-7% deep shadows and were brighter/more saturated than Heroes of Bronze shots of the same mood; the film look comes from moody contrast
- **Applies to:** all

## r11 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Music only at chosen moments (opening hook, tension builds, battles/climaxes, emotional beats, ending), never wall-to-wall; other scenes run on ambience + sound effects; fade music in/out on story beats
- **Why:** owner 2026-11-19: no background music for the whole video; film practice: music means more when it arrives
- **Applies to:** edit, shots

## r12 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Character dialogue may be DRAMATISED (imagined) to make moments stronger: it must fit the real history (right person, real situation, his known character, no anachronisms) and never change what happened; tag invented lines [dramatised], real quotes [source: ...]; narrator facts stay strictly sourced; description gets one line 'Some dialogue is dramatised.'; no fixed number of character lines (wherever they fit, r1)
- **Why:** owner 2026-11-19: dialogue does not have to be fact-checked, other creators dramatise too; keep narrator facts accurate for trust
- **Applies to:** script, package

## r13 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** No full video render (long or Short) until the 3D library is proven at the quality target (~85% of the Heroes of Bronze reference, judged on stills + tc look); until then only test stills and short tests
- **Why:** owner 2026-11-19: once we are sure we hit the quality we want, we run
- **Applies to:** render

## r14 (2026-11-20)
- **Rule:** Maps follow the Epic History TV First Crusade reference (docs/MAP_STYLE.md): aged parchment 3D map, tilted low camera with slow drift, territory fills with emblem patterns, serif labels, banner army blocks, animated arrows
- **Why:** Owner loves every part of how that reference looks and shows maps (2026-11-20)
- **Applies to:** shots

## r15 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** No AI images ever. Visuals are 2D cartoon illustrations drawn in code, aiming at the videoplayback(13) Japan-history reference: thick outlines, round-headed cast, warm colours, rich detailed sets
- **Why:** Owner dropped 3D on 2026-11-21 and chose code-drawn 2D over AI images
- **Applies to:** shots

## r16 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** Cast style v1 approved: spike/art_dir/cast_sheet.png (+ cast_lib.js). Round head ~40% of height, small dot eyes, short thick brows, thick warm-brown outline, flat colour + one soft shade, costume carries the era, held props really gripped
- **Why:** Owner said 'thats fine' to cast sheet v1 on 2026-11-21
- **Applies to:** shots

## r17 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** Never reuse scenes: every video gets its own freshly drawn sets, backgrounds, compositions and props. Only the style kit is shared (outline/shading helpers, head + face construction, motion helpers, renderer)
- **Why:** Owner 2026-11-21: 'i dont wanna reuse scenes' (same principle as CapitalCode r82/r83)
- **Applies to:** shots, build

## r18 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** Draw it right the first time: aim ABOVE the videoplayback(13) reference, not equal. Every scene is drawn by following a written drawing standard (art bible) before any code, then the agent checks every still against a production-grade checklist and fixes it itself before the owner ever sees it. No draw-show-fix loops with the owner
- **Why:** Owner 2026-11-21: 'we can still be better than reference. i dont wanna just draw and fix... draw it perfect the first time... the agent checks the still if they look production grade ready'
- **Applies to:** shots, build

## r19 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** Each character's identity stays consistent across shots and videos: one saved character sheet per person (face, hair, beard, build, colours, signature costume); scenes around them are always new
- **Why:** Owner 2026-11-21: 'each character identity stays consistent'
- **Applies to:** shots, build

## r20 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** Fully autonomous: the owner does not approve topics, titles, scripts, stills or renders. The agent decides everything and ships production-ready videos; every decision is ranked by views, subscribers, watch hours and retention
- **Why:** Owner 2026-11-21: 'i dont wanna get involved... main goal is to get views, subscribers, watch hours and retention'
- **Applies to:** topic, packaging, script, shots

## r21 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** Thumbnails are drawn fresh in code for every video (same 2D style): rich topic background, the video's characters with strong emotion, one striking object, <=3 big words; built to win the click (CTR first)
- **Why:** Owner 2026-11-21: 'thumbnails drawn fresh by the system for each video... most important thing is to get clicks'
- **Applies to:** packaging

## r22 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** Delivery = upload pack (video, title, description, tags, chapters, thumbnail, best posting time); owner uploads by hand in YouTube Studio. No API upload
- **Why:** Owner chose option B on 2026-11-21
- **Applies to:** package

## r23 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** Within one video, the same real place keeps one set design (drawn once for that video) and every shot there is a new composition/angle/action/time of day; a different place is a different set built from scratch. Never reuse sets between videos
- **Why:** Owner 2026-11-21: 'if that event happens in the same place, we can go with your pick. but if the place is different, different scene and everything build from scratch'
- **Applies to:** shots, build

## r24 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** Great storytelling first: every video is a story, not a list of facts. Before any script line, design the story: one main character with a want and a fear, a central dramatic question, rising stakes, turning points, a twist, an emotional ending. Script follows docs/SCRIPT_BIBLE.md and must score >= 90/100 on the story self-check or it is rewritten
- **Why:** Owner 2026-11-21: 'the main important thing is to focus for quality, views, and retention... we need great storytelling'
- **Applies to:** script

## r25 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** Topic choice scores STORY POTENTIAL as well as demand: a clear main character, a real dilemma, rising stakes, a twist, strong visual moments, room for character dialogue. A topic with demand but a weak story is rejected
- **Why:** Owner 2026-11-21: 'before anything agent will have to pick a topic and consider about storytelling, how can he build the story to be interesting and entertaining'
- **Applies to:** topic

## r26 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** Every named character has one unique voice (Fish) saved with their character sheet and used in every video; dialogue goes where it lifts the scene; cinematic sound effects in every scene; music ONLY at peak moments: opening, ending, battles/marching, tension peaks, emotional scenes
- **Why:** Owner 2026-11-21: 'characters dialogue with their unique voiceovers and cinematic sound effects and music... only when peak moments, emotional scenes, opening, ending, marching'
- **Applies to:** script, voice, edit

## r27 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** After the script is written, a separate VIEWER agent (fresh, never saw the story page or writing) reads/listens to it as a first-time viewer and judges: where they would click away, get bored or confused, what they feel, whether they would watch to the end and subscribe. Verdict GO or NO-GO with notes; NO-GO goes back to the writer. Nothing is drawn before GO
- **Why:** Owner 2026-11-21: 'an agent will view the script as a viewer and decide if its good enough to go production yet or not'
- **Applies to:** script

## r28 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** Everything is synced to the voiceover: cuts on word/pause boundaries, each action, gesture and expression starts on the word that names it, character mouths follow their own dialogue, sound effects hit on their word
- **Why:** Owner 2026-11-21: 'everything scenes and shots including characters acting will be synced to voiceover'
- **Applies to:** shots, build, edit

## r29 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** Sync must feel natural and cinematic, never abrupt or mechanical: actions ease in/out with anticipation and follow-through, start a few frames BEFORE their word so they land on it, overlap (head before body, cloth after), characters keep breathing/idling between beats, cuts are motivated (look, movement, sound leading in: J/L cuts), camera moves start and end softly, music and sfx fade rather than snap
- **Why:** Owner 2026-11-21: 'everything must have natural flow, feels cinematic natural feeling. not abrupt or anything'
- **Applies to:** shots, build, edit

## r30 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** Like CapitalCode: the picture acts out the narration in real time. Every meaningful phrase makes something visibly happen on screen (an action, a reaction, an object appearing or changing, a camera move or a cut), roughly every 1-3 s; never one still picture held under a long stretch of narration. Movement comes from the story beats, not from random looping motion
- **Why:** Owner 2026-11-21: 'like capitalcode, scenes, shots and things happen synced on voiceover'
- **Applies to:** shots, build

## r31 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** The product is a cinematic 2D animated FILM-like video: it must feel like watching an animated film (acting, camera, light, sound, music, editing), never a slideshow of illustrations
- **Why:** Owner 2026-11-21: 'our goal is cinematic 2D animated film like video'
- **Applies to:** all

## r32 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** Era lock: clothing, armour, weapons, hairstyles, buildings, furniture, ships, tools, plants, animals and landscapes all match the exact time and place of the video. Each video's era sheet lists every visual item with a source (museum pieces, archaeology, period art); every shot is checked for anachronisms before render. Myths are drawn in the era that told them (Greek gods in Archaic/Classical Greek dress and temples)
- **Why:** Owner 2026-11-21: 'clothings, building, environments, and everything should match the era the video is talking about' (strengthens r4)
- **Applies to:** research, shots, build

## r33 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** Story design is not a fixed 3-part formula: want/fear/choice is only the minimum for the main character. For each topic the agent picks, from the WHOLE real story, every element that makes it gripping: other characters (rival, villain, ally, traitor, ensemble), secrets, betrayals, ticking clocks, mysteries, irony, sacrifices, transformations, moral dilemmas, rise-and-fall, revenge, consequences and legacy; and the story shape that fits the topic (mystery investigation, rise and fall, countdown, heist, multi-character, revenge)
- **Why:** Owner 2026-11-21: 'not only 3 things, could be many more based on the whole history story and topic'
- **Applies to:** topic, script

## r34 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** Full film language in every video: colour grading per era, act and mood; a camera vocabulary (smooth zoom in/out, push, pull, pan, tilt, track, crane, gentle handheld in battle, rack focus) chosen for each beat's meaning; shot sizes and angles for emotion and power; editing with rhythm (wide to medium to close, faster in action, slower in dread), match cuts, dissolves, whip pans, fades to black between acts, montage and cross-cutting
- **Why:** Owner 2026-11-21: 'color grading, camera movements, zoom ins, zoom outs, editing??'
- **Applies to:** shots, build, edit

## r35 (2026-11-21)
- **Rule:** Maps stay in the Epic History look (docs/MAP_STYLE.md: aged parchment, tilted low camera with slow drift, territory fills with emblem patterns, serif labels, scroll city tags, banner armies, self-drawing arrows, spotlight, battle maps with unit blocks) but are DRAWN IN 2D CODE in the renderer (no Blender): the flat map is tilted with a 3D perspective transform, far edge blurred; little figures, ships and banners on the map use our cast style; maps alternate with scenes every 5-20 s. Replaces the Blender part of r14
- **Why:** Owner 2026-11-21 asked 'what about maps' after dropping 3D
- **Applies to:** shots, build
