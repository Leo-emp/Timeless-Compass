# Timeless Compass owner rules

Read before every stage (`python -m tc rules --stage <stage>`).

## r1 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Narrator only: characters act but never speak; famous quotes are spoken by the narrator
- **Why:** AI character voices sounded unnatural (owner test 2026-11-19)
- **Applies to:** script, voice

## r2 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Serious, dramatic, mysterious tone; never funny or cheesy
- **Why:** AI voices can't do comedy; serious fits history + secret projects
- **Applies to:** script

## r3 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** No on-screen captions or speech bubbles; only place/date cards and map labels
- **Why:** the voice tells the story, like the reference films
- **Applies to:** shots, edit

## r4 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Era accuracy: costumes, armour, gods, soldiers, buildings and landscapes must match the exact time and place
- **Why:** owner requirement; wrong-era details break trust
- **Applies to:** all

## r5 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Growth first: every choice (topic, title, thumbnail, hook, pacing) is ranked by views, CTR and retention
- **Why:** owner priority from day one
- **Applies to:** all

## r6 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Real people are shown through costume, setting and a name card, never as exact face copies
- **Why:** avoid deepfake-style lookalikes
- **Applies to:** shots, build

## r7 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Zero spend: free tiers and free tools only; check pricing before using any paid feature
- **Why:** owner does not want to spend money
- **Applies to:** all

## r8 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Laptop stays light: big files go to R2/cloud; delete local job files after upload
- **Why:** laptop has little disk space
- **Applies to:** render, archive

## r9 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Never copy other productions (e.g. Heroes of Bronze): their frames are only a quality ruler. Character faces, costumes, shield art, shot compositions and colour style are designed from historical sources (busts, vase paintings, museum pieces) and our own settings
- **Why:** owner: we must not copy them; our look must be our own
- **Applies to:** build, shots, library

## r10 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Every shot passes 'tc look' (score >= 80 vs the closest-mood reference frames): keep real deep shadows (3-17% of the frame near black), do not over-brighten faces, keep dusk warmth/saturation inside the reference range
- **Why:** measured 2026-11-19: our renders had 0-7% deep shadows and were brighter/more saturated than Heroes of Bronze shots of the same mood; the film look comes from moody contrast
- **Applies to:** all

## r11 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Music only at chosen moments (opening hook, tension builds, battles/climaxes, emotional beats, ending), never wall-to-wall; other scenes run on ambience + sound effects; fade music in/out on story beats
- **Why:** owner 2026-11-19: no background music for the whole video; film practice: music means more when it arrives
- **Applies to:** edit, shots

## r12 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** Character dialogue may be DRAMATISED (imagined) to make moments stronger: it must fit the real history (right person, real situation, his known character, no anachronisms) and never change what happened; tag invented lines [dramatised], real quotes [source: ...]; narrator facts stay strictly sourced; description gets one line 'Some dialogue is dramatised.'; 1-2 character lines per video
- **Why:** owner 2026-11-19: dialogue does not have to be fact-checked, other creators dramatise too; keep narrator facts accurate for trust
- **Applies to:** script, package

## r13 (2026-11-19)
- **Rule:** No full video render (long or Short) until the 3D library is proven at the quality target (~85% of the Heroes of Bronze reference, judged on stills + tc look); until then only test stills and short tests
- **Why:** owner 2026-11-19: once we are sure we hit the quality we want, we run
- **Applies to:** render
