## hook
Before the Persians came, Athens asked the god at Delphi how to survive. The answer was: run. [src: f-oracle-first] {loop}
## build
The priestess Aristonice told the envoys to flee to the ends of the earth. [src: f-oracle-first]
They came back as suppliants, olive branches in their hands, and begged again. [src: f-oracle-second]
ARISTONICE (artemisia): "Far-seeing Zeus grants a wooden wall alone to stand unsacked."  [source: Herodotus, Histories 7.141]
ARISTONICE (artemisia): "Divine Salamis, you will destroy the children of women."  [source: Herodotus, Histories 7.141]
But what was the wooden wall? Old men said the thorn hedge around the Acropolis. [src: f-oracle-hedge] {loop}
Themistocles said it was the ships. If Athenians were meant to die at Salamis, the god would have called it cruel, not divine. [src: f-oracle-ships]
## payoff
Athens believed him. The families left, and the men went to the ships. [src: f-oracle-vote, f-evacuation]
The few who trusted a timber wall on the Acropolis died when the Persians took it. [src: f-defenders, f-burned] {beat}
The real wooden wall was waiting at Salamis. [src: f-oracle-ships, f-narrows]
